## 1 · @rourke (Rourke Sefton-Minns, ~927K) — „Result first → 3 Steps → Comment AI“

**Formel:** Ergebnis in Sekunde 0 zeigen → „this is AI“ → Tool nennen → 3 schnelle Schritte → Payoff nochmal → Signatur-CTA mit Keyword (ManyChat-DM).

### Skript 1A — „Put yourself inside any world“
- **0–2s (Bild: du läufst durch eine fotoreale 3D-Stadt):** „None of this is real. Not the street, not the light — only me.“
- **2–6s:** „I filmed this in my room in Erfurt. Here's how.“
- **6–20s (Screen-Recording, 3 Schritte):**
  „Step one: film yourself on a plain wall — phone is enough.
  Step two: build the world in Blender, rough blocks, no detail.
  Step three: Seedance 2.5 turns the blocks into a real place — and keeps you in it.“
- **20–26s (Vorher/Nachher-Split):** „Blocks in. Film out. That's the whole trick.“
- **CTA:** „Comment WORLD and I'll send you the full prompt pack.“

### Skript 1B — „Film VFX without a VFX team“
- **0–2s:** „This shot would've cost a VFX studio a week.“
- **2–5s:** „I did it in one afternoon — with one AI tool.“
- **5–20s:** „Take any real clip. Tell the AI what to add — fire, rain, a crowd. It layers it *onto* your footage, not instead of it. Your camera move stays. Your face stays. Only the world changes.“
- **20–25s:** „Filmmakers, this is the part nobody's talking about.“
- **CTA:** „Comment VFX and I'll send you the link.“

## 3 · @ohneis652 (Andries Ohneisser, ~404K) — „Contrarian Command → Tool erledigt es → Frage → Follow“

**Formel:** Befehls-Hook („Stop doing X“) → „Just do Y and let the tool handle the rest“ → Split-Screen, Cuts alle 1–2 Sek., kurze Text-Overlays → Engagement-Frage → „Follow for more“. Ästhetisch, Art-Director-Blick, wenig Worte.

### Skript 3A — „Stop writing style prompts“
- **0–2s (Split: dein Foto / 12 Filter-Varianten im Raster):** „Stop writing style prompts.“
- **2–6s:** „Just upload a photo and give it one material.“
- **6–16s (Cut pro Material, Text-Overlay nur das Wort):** „Chrome. Mosaic. Glass. Marble. Neon. Thermal.“
- **16–20s:** „Same photo. Twelve worlds. Zero prompt engineering.“
- **Frage:** „Which one would you wear?“
- **CTA:** „Comment TASTE for all twelve — follow for more.“ *(nutzt dein fertiges Filter-Sheet)*

### Skript 3B — „Stop moodboarding on Pinterest“
- **0–2s:** „Stop building moodboards on Pinterest.“
- **2–6s:** „Give the AI one reference — it builds the whole visual identity.“
- **6–16s (Cuts):** „Palette. Type. Lighting. Logo on a wall. Logo on a shirt.“
- **16–19s:** „That's a brand in ten minutes.“
- **Frage:** „Would you use this for a client?“
- **CTA:** „Follow for more AI art direction.“
